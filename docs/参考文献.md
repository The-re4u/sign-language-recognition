[36] Fraunhofer Institute, "3D Geometric Features Based Real-Time American Sign Language Recognition Using PointNet and MLP with MediaPipe Hand Skeleton Detection," *Fraunhofer Publica*, 2025.
> **本文定位**：与本文新方案高度一致的独立工作。该论文从 MediaPipe 21 关键点提取手指关节角度等 3D 几何特征，经 MLP 编码后与 PointNet 特征融合，在 ASL 字母+数字上达到 99.81%，推理 <10ms——其"几何特征 MLP 编码 + 深度学习特征融合"的技术路线与本文 HandShapeContext + CrossModalFusion 的设计理念直接对应，为本文几何描述子替代 CNN 视觉特征提供了独立验证。

[37] M. Pu, M. K. Lim, C. Y. Chong, and C. C. Loy, "Sigma: Semantically Informative Pre-training for Skeleton-based Sign Language Understanding," *arXiv preprint arXiv:2509.21223*, 2025.
> **本文定位**：纯骨架多模态表征学习的最新进展。Sigma 完全基于骨架关键点，通过 Sign-aware Early Fusion（双向交叉注意力）融合骨架视觉特征与文本语义，在 WLASL、CSL-Daily 等 4 个 benchmark 上达到 SOTA——从多模态表征学习的角度验证了"从单一骨架数据源派生多元表征"这一本文核心设计思路的可行性。

[38] R. Wong, N. C. Camgöz, and R. Bowden, "SignRep: Enhancing Self-Supervised Sign Representations," in *Proc. IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*, 2025.
> **本文定位**：Image+Skeleton 多模态融合的权威范式。Surrey 组（Camgöz/Bowden 是 SLR 领域最被认可的团队之一）提出用骨架关键点作为预训练先验指导 RGB Masked Autoencoder 学习，在推理时不再需要骨架输入。该工作直接证明了"Image模态 + Skeleton模态"的双源融合有效性——本文 E4 方案的 CNN(RGB ROI) + SpatialGCN(MediaPipe骨架) + GatedFusion(门控融合) 正是对这一范式的工程实现。

[39] H. Hu, W. Zhao, W. Zhou, and H. Li, "SignBERT+: Hand-Model-Aware Self-Supervised Pre-Training for Sign Language Understanding," *IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)*, vol. 45, no. 9, pp. 11221–11239, 2023.
> **本文定位**：国内 SLR 最权威团队（中科大 周文罡/李厚强）的 TPAMI 期刊论文。SignBERT+ 将手部先验知识融入多模态自监督预训练，在多个 SLR benchmark 上达到 SOTA。为本文"从 MediaPipe 手部关键点派生结构化先验"的技术路线提供权威支撑。

[40] R. Zuo, F. Wei, and B. Mak, "NLA-SLR: Natural Language-Assisted Sign Language Recognition," in *Proc. IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)*, 2023.
> **本文定位**：CVPR 顶会论文，展示自然语言语义 + RGB + 骨架三模态融合的有效性。为本文引入语义模态（手势标签→文本嵌入）提供直接文献支撑。

[41] Y. Zhou, Z. Xia, Y. Chen, C. Neidle, and D. N. Metaxas, "A Multimodal Spatio-Temporal GCN Model with Enhancements for Isolated Sign Recognition," in *Proc. LREC-COLING Workshop on Sign Language Resources*, 2024.
> **本文定位**：Rutgers 大学 Metaxas 教授组的多模态 GCN 工作。采用骨架+手形双流输入 + 门控模块（per-channel multi-layer convolutions）筛选关键帧——其"门控模块"设计理念与本文 GatedFusion（逐维门控 CNN↔几何特征）直接对应，为本文融合机制提供独立验证。

[42] Y. Chen, Z. Zhang, C. Yuan, B. Li, Y. Deng, and W. Hu, "Channel-wise Topology Refinement Graph Convolution for Skeleton-Based Action Recognition," in *Proc. IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*, 2021, pp. 13359–13368.
> **本文定位**：骨架 GCN 领域引用量 1000+ 的权威基线。CTR-GCN 通过通道级拓扑细化学习动态图结构——本文 SpatialGCN 的手部优化拓扑（指尖全连接+指内跳跃+指间交叉连接）即受其启发，提供了骨架建模的权威方法论基础。

[43] J. Arevalo, T. Solorio, M. Montes-y-Gómez, and F. A. González, "Gated Multimodal Units for Information Fusion," in *Proc. International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) Workshop*, 2017.
> **本文定位**：门控多模态融合的经典文献。提出的 Gated Multimodal Unit 是本文 GatedFusion（gate = σ(MLP([cnn; geo_proj])), vis = gate·cnn + (1-gate)·geo_proj）的理论源头，为门控机制的设计选择提供文献依据。
